# Revenue Overview Table

This table provides an overview of daily revenue generated by various DeFi protocols.

## Schema

*   `total24h`: BIGINT - Total revenue in the last 24 hours.
*   `total48hto24h`: BIGINT - Total revenue from 48 to 24 hours ago.
*   `total7d`: DOUBLE - Total revenue in the last 7 days.
*   `total14dto7d`: DOUBLE - Total revenue from 14 to 7 days ago.
*   `total60dto30d`: DOUBLE - Total revenue from 60 to 30 days ago.
*   `total30d`: DOUBLE - Total revenue in the last 30 days.
*   `total1y`: DOUBLE - Total revenue in the last 1 year.
*   `totalAllTime`: BIGINT - Total revenue all-time.
*   `average1y`: DOUBLE - Average daily revenue over 1 year.
*   `change_30dover30d`: DOUBLE - Percentage change in revenue over the last 30 days compared to the previous 30 days.
*   `breakdown24h`: MAP(VARCHAR, MAP(VARCHAR, INTEGER)) - Breakdown of revenue in the last 24 hours by chain and token.
*   `breakdown30d`: MAP(VARCHAR, MAP(VARCHAR, INTEGER)) - Breakdown of revenue in the last 30 days by chain and token.
*   `total7DaysAgo`: DOUBLE - Total revenue 7 days ago.
*   `total30DaysAgo`: DOUBLE - Total revenue 30 days ago.
*   `defillamaId`: VARCHAR - DeFiLlama protocol ID.
*   `name`: VARCHAR - Name of the protocol.
*   `displayName`: VARCHAR - Display name of the protocol.
*   `module`: VARCHAR - Internal module name.
*   `category`: VARCHAR - Category of the protocol.
*   `logo`: VARCHAR - URL to the protocol's logo.
*   `chains`: VARCHAR[] - Array of chains the protocol is available on.
*   `protocolType`: VARCHAR - Type of protocol (e.g., "protocol", "cex").
*   `methodologyURL`: VARCHAR - URL to the methodology description.
*   `methodology`: VARCHAR - Description of the data collection methodology.
*   `latestFetchIsOk`: BOOLEAN - Indicates if the latest data fetch was successful.
*   `slug`: VARCHAR - URL-friendly name.
*   `id`: VARCHAR - Unique identifier for the protocol.
*   `change_1d`: DOUBLE - Percentage change in revenue over 1 day.
*   `change_7d`: DOUBLE - Percentage change in revenue over 7 days.
*   `change_1m`: DOUBLE - Percentage change in revenue over 1 month.
*   `change_7dover7d`: DOUBLE - Percentage change in revenue over the last 7 days compared to the previous 7 days.
*   `parentProtocol`: VARCHAR - ID of the parent protocol, if applicable.
*   `linkedProtocols`: VARCHAR[] - Array of linked protocols.
*   `disabled`: BOOLEAN - Indicates if the protocol is disabled.
